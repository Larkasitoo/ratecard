Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

RateCard is a free brand-deal pricing estimator for creators. This policy explains what we do — and don't — collect.

What we collect

Practically nothing. To generate an estimate you send us: a niche, a follower count, a platform, a content type, and optionally an engagement rate. These inputs are processed in memory to compute your estimate and are not stored, logged with identifiers, or tied to you.

  • No accounts. No sign-ups. No passwords.
  • No cookies, no tracking pixels, no analytics identifiers.
  • No payment information (the service is free).

Server logs

Our hosting provider may keep standard, short-lived web server logs (IP address, timestamp, requested path) for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to identify or profile users.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with anyone, because we don't collect personal data in the first place.

Children

RateCard is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated date.
